Northstar workshop guide

The current workshop starts from a complete financial dashboard. Follow one investigation through the overview, a region or category, and an individual order.

Read the current learner guide Open the dashboard
  1. Email: click a KPI and choose Share finding; review and send its metric and current filter context.
  2. Trello: click a region/category bar and choose Assign investigation for that selected segment.
  3. Supabase: open order Details, edit a numeric cell inline, review and confirm the correction, then reload and check the recalculated metrics.
  4. GitHub Issue: choose Report data issue from the record row or its audit context.

Clone the supplied app, deploy your own copy, customize its design or case, and configure your own service accounts. The action handlers are included. An attempt without the required configuration explains what is missing.
